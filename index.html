Prototype rules

  1. Gold, Blue, and Red move clockwise.
  2. Pieces use familiar chess movement on this prototype board.
  3. Capture an enemy king to eliminate that commander.
  4. The defeated army immediately comes under your control.
  5. You may move one piece from any army you command each turn.
  6. The last surviving king wins.

Castling and en passant are not included in this first build. Pawns promote automatically to queens.
